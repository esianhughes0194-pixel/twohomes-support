Subscription

TwoHomes Plus offers optional premium features on top of the free plan (which includes the full shared custody calendar, schedule change requests, unlimited children with name and birthdate, and text messaging). A subscription is not required to use TwoHomes Plus.

Plans. Monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions are available, each with a free trial period. Pricing and the exact trial length are shown before purchase, on the in-app upgrade screen and in the App Store purchase confirmation.

Billing & cancellation. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription any time in your device's Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Family sharing. One subscription unlocks premium features for the whole family — both parents linked to the same TwoHomes Plus family get access, at no extra cost, regardless of which parent purchased it.

Subscriptions are processed by Apple; TwoHomes Plus does not receive or store your payment card details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status across devices — see the Privacy Policy below for what that involves.

Account & data deletion

Delete your account in the app. Open Settings (the gear icon at the top right of your Dashboard) and tap Delete account. This removes your account and your personal profile.

Shared family records. Messages, schedule, expenses and photos are shared between you and your co-parent, so they stay available to your co-parent when you delete your own account. To have a family's shared records deleted, or to have a child's account deleted, email esianhughes0194@gmail.com and we'll do it.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

What we collect. To provide the app, TwoHomes Plus stores: your name and email (for your account); your family's custody schedule and calendar events; expense records you log (amounts, descriptions, categories, and who they're split between), including receipt photos attached to reimbursement requests, and any recurring expenses you set up; handover packing lists; travel-abroad consent records (which child is travelling, the destination, dates, flight and accommodation details, an emergency contact, and each parent's consent); messages you send within the app, including photos shared in messages; information you choose to add about your children (name, birthdate, and — for children with full profiles — doctor's surgery, allergies, medicines, and activities); a private journal that only its author can read; your device's push notification token if you turn on notifications; and your subscription entitlement status. If you give someone else access (see below), we store their name and the access you granted, plus their email address if they sign in with one. We do not collect location data, and we do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

How it's used. This data is used solely to provide the app's features to you and the co-parent(s), children and other people linked to your family. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. The only third parties that handle it are the service providers described below.

Where it's stored. Data is stored and processed using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore for records, Cloud Storage for photos, and Cloud Functions for server-side features), which acts as our data processor. Access to your family's data is restricted, at the database level, to the accounts linked to that family, and each kind of account sees only what it needs (see "Who can see your family's information" below) — no other TwoHomes Plus user can read it. Our providers may process data outside the UK, including in the United States.

Who can see your family's information. Parents linked to the family can see everything in it, except that each parent's private journal is visible only to them. A child's account can see the shared custody calendar and their own message thread with their parents (see "Children's accounts"). Family members you invite, such as a grandparent, can see the custody calendar only. If you invite a solicitor, mediator or CAFCASS officer, they get read-only access to the custody schedule, schedule-change requests, and the messages between you and your co-parent — and nothing else: not expenses, children's profiles, health information, travel-consent records or journals. Where two parents are linked, both must approve before a professional's access code works, and either parent can remove their access at any time from Settings.

Receipt scanning. If you choose "Scan a receipt" when adding an expense (a TwoHomes Plus subscriber feature), the photo you take is sent, via our server, to Google's Gemini AI service, which reads the shop name, date and total. The app asks for your permission the first time you use it. Only the photo is sent — not your name, email or any family details. We do not store the scan photo on our servers unless you also attach it to a reimbursement request, in which case it is stored like any other receipt photo. We record only a count of scans per family per day, to limit misuse. Google handles the photo under its own terms — see policies.google.com/privacy.

Notifications. If you turn on notifications, we store your device's push token so we can alert you to new messages and schedule requests. These notifications are delivered through Apple's push service and Expo's push notification service, which receive your push token and the text of the notification (such as the sender's name and the start of a message). You can turn each type off in Settings.

Messages. Messages cannot be edited or deleted once sent, so that co-parents have a reliable record of what was said. They are removed only when a family's data is deleted (see "Account & data deletion" above).

Exports and court bundles. When you export a court bundle, message history or expense report, the PDF is created on your device from your family's records and you choose where to share it. TwoHomes Plus does not receive a copy. These exports are records of what you and your co-parent entered and did in the app; we cannot verify that any of it is true, and they are not legal advice.

Subscriptions. Subscription purchases and renewal status are processed by Apple and managed on our behalf by RevenueCat, a subscription infrastructure provider. RevenueCat receives an anonymous purchaser identifier and your purchase/renewal history in order to unlock premium features for your family; it does not receive your payment card details, which Apple handles directly. See RevenueCat's own privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy for details on their processing.

Children's accounts. A child can only get their own TwoHomes Plus account if a parent on that family invites them and explicitly confirms they are that child's parent or guardian. A child can sign in with an email address or, if they don't have one, with a login code and a 4-digit PIN that their parent chooses; the PIN is stored only as a salted, one-way hash, and repeated wrong attempts temporarily lock sign-in. A child's account is limited to viewing the shared custody calendar and messaging their parents — it has no access to financial records, health records, or schedule-change requests. A parent can request deletion of their child's account at any time via the support email above.

Voice calls. Calls between co-parents connect directly device-to-device (WebRTC); TwoHomes Plus does not record or store call audio. A connectivity relay service may be used to help establish the connection on some networks, which can see that a call is occurring but not its content.

Your choices. You can delete your own account in the app (Settings → Delete account), and you can request a copy of your data, or deletion of your family's shared records, at any time by emailing the support address above.

Changes to this policy. If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
