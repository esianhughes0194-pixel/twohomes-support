Subscription

TwoHomes Plus offers optional premium features on top of the free plan (which includes the full shared custody calendar, schedule change requests, unlimited children with name and birthdate, and text messaging). A subscription is not required to use TwoHomes Plus.

Plans. Monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions are available, each with a free trial period. Pricing and the exact trial length are shown before purchase, on the in-app upgrade screen and in the App Store purchase confirmation.

Billing & cancellation. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription any time in your device's Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Family sharing. One subscription unlocks premium features for the whole family — both parents linked to the same TwoHomes Plus family get access, at no extra cost, regardless of which parent purchased it.

Subscriptions are processed by Apple; TwoHomes Plus does not receive or store your payment card details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status across devices — see the Privacy Policy below for what that involves.

Account & data deletion

To delete your account or your family's data, email the support address above from the email address associated with your TwoHomes Plus account and we'll process the request. If you invited a child's account, let us know and we'll remove that account and its data too.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect. To provide the app, TwoHomes Plus stores: your name and email (for your account); your family's custody schedule and calendar events; expense records you log (amounts, descriptions, categories, and who they're split between), including receipt photos attached to reimbursement requests; messages you send within the app, including photos shared in messages; information you choose to add about your children (name, birthdate, and — for children with full profiles — doctor's surgery, allergies, medicines, and activities); and your subscription entitlement status. We do not collect location data, and we do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

How it's used. All of this data is used solely to provide the app's features to you and the co-parent(s) and children linked to your family. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Where it's stored. Data is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore for records, Cloud Storage for photos), which acts as our data processor. Access to your family's data is restricted, at the database level, to the specific accounts linked to that family — no other TwoHomes Plus user can read it.

Subscriptions. Subscription purchases and renewal status are processed by Apple and managed on our behalf by RevenueCat, a subscription infrastructure provider. RevenueCat receives an anonymous purchaser identifier and your purchase/renewal history in order to unlock premium features for your family; it does not receive your payment card details, which Apple handles directly. See RevenueCat's own privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy for details on their processing.

Children's accounts. A child can only get their own TwoHomes Plus account if a parent on that family invites them and explicitly confirms they are that child's parent or guardian. A child's account is limited to viewing the shared custody calendar and messaging their parents — it has no access to financial records, health records, or schedule-change requests. A parent can request deletion of their child's account at any time via the support email above.

Voice calls. Calls between co-parents connect directly device-to-device (WebRTC); TwoHomes Plus does not record or store call audio. A connectivity relay service may be used to help establish the connection on some networks, which can see that a call is occurring but not its content.

Your choices. You can request a copy of your data or full deletion of your account and associated family data at any time by emailing the support address above.

Changes to this policy. If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
